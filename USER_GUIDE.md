# Running Wild — User Guide

## For Students

### Step 1: Open the App

Your instructor will share a link. Open it in any browser on your phone, tablet, or laptop.

### Step 2: Choose "Student"

On the landing page, tap **Join Game**.

### Step 3: Enter the Game Code

Your instructor will display a 4-digit game code on the projector. Type it in, then enter your team name.

### Step 4: Select Your Backpack

You will see a list of items grouped by category: Shelter, Clothing, Tools, Food, Water, Medical, and so on. Each item shows its weight and volume. Food items also show their calorie content; water items show their volume in litres.

Check the items you want to bring. Two progress bars at the bottom track your total weight and volume against the limits (20 kg and 80 L). You cannot submit if either bar exceeds 100%.

Think carefully about trade-offs. You need enough food and water to survive 50 days, but you also need items that protect you from weather, injuries, and equipment failures.

### Step 5: Choose Your Location Card

Select the location card your team drew at the start of the session. Each location has different environmental conditions that affect gameplay.

### Step 6: Submit

Tap **Submit backpack**. You will see a confirmation screen. From this point, your selections are locked. Wait for the instructor to start the game.

### Step 7: Watch the Game

The instructor will run the 50-day simulation on the projector. Each day, random events occur. Your HP, water, and food will change based on what you packed. If your HP reaches zero, your water runs out for 3 consecutive days, or your food runs out for 10 consecutive days, your team is eliminated.

At the end of 50 days, surviving teams receive bonus HP from any leftover water and food. The team with the highest final HP wins.

## For the Instructor

### Creating a Game

1. Open the app and choose **Create / Manage Game**.
2. Set a game seed (any number; the same seed always produces the same event sequence).
3. Click **Create Game**. A 4-digit code will appear on screen.
4. Share this code with your students.

### Lobby Phase

The lobby page refreshes automatically. As students submit their backpacks, their team names and loadout summaries appear on your screen. Wait until all teams have submitted.

### Starting the Game

Click **Start Game**. The app will assign a random trait card to each team and generate the 50-day event calendar. Trait cards are drawn after item selection, so students cannot plan around them.

### Running the Simulation

Click **Next Day** to advance. Each day, the app displays the event that occurred and shows every team's HP change, current HP, remaining water, and remaining food. Eliminated teams are greyed out.

An expandable HP History chart tracks all teams over time.

### Scoreboard

After day 50 (or when all teams are eliminated), the app shows final rankings with end-game bonuses from leftover resources.

### Rejoining

If you close the browser during a game, reopen the app, choose Instructor, and enter the game code under "Rejoin existing game" to resume.

## Quick Reference

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Starting HP | 30 |
| Starting water | 8 L |
| Starting food | 8000 kcal |
| Weight limit | 20 kg |
| Volume limit | 80 L |
| Daily water consumption | 0.3 L |
| Daily food consumption | 400 kcal |
| Dehydration elimination | 3 consecutive days at 0 L water |
| Starvation elimination | 10 consecutive days below 300 kcal |
| End-game water bonus | 1 HP per litre (max 5 HP) |
| End-game food bonus | 1 HP per 1000 kcal (max 10 HP) |
